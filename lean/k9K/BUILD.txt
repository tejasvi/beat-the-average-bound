run_shards.py compiles each module with `lean -j1 -o … -i …` (SHARD_ILEAN=0 skips the .ilean) under an
address-space cap (RLIMIT_AS = SHARD_MEM_GB). The cap must be large: a -j1 lean process runs about one thread per
theorem of the module, each with a ~1 GB address-space reservation, and the kernel's cache tables reserve more; under
small caps thread creation fails with "failed to create thread: Resource temporarily unavailable". Kernel memory grows
within a theorem (~0.1 GB per second of kernel time in stage 3), which is why stage-3 theorems are kept under ~2 min
and join theorems under ~5 min. MALLOC_ARENA_MAX=1: Lean checks each theorem on its own thread, and with per-thread
malloc arenas the freed caches of earlier theorems are not reused (a 9-theorem row shard peaked at 26 GB with one arena,
over 52 GB without). Do not `lake build BeatAvg.K.K9`: it would compile the shards with one thread per
core each, in parallel, and recompile the shards compiled outside Lake.
